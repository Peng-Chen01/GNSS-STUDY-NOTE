What aspects that can be improved that can enhance the quality of GNSS?
$\sigma _x = GDOP * \sigma _{UERE}$
From the formula, it can be reducing the measurement errors and improving satellite geometry.

For GDOP
- Pseudo satellite(pseudolite), a terrestrial transmitter able to emit differential corrections modulated on GNSS-like signal. This approach can reduce GDOP coefficient and increase service availability. However, the coverage area is limited and due to near-far resistance it may act like a jammer.
For $\sigma _{UERE}$
- Differential GNSS: broadcast error to user that can eliminate some errors and improve accuracy and precision.
- Two augmentation systems: Ground Based Augmentation Systems(GBAS) and space Based Augmentation Systems(SBAS)

  How GBAS works:
  There are Reference Stations which have precise positions that are well known, they will compare the estimated position with true position and broadcast the error to users.

  Usually, use the correction of pseudo range approach, from the formulae it can be observed that space error and control error can be eliminated. If using Double Difference(DD), even can cancel the receiver clock error.

   Limitations: The broadcast of correction of errors may be a little bit out-of-date due to propagation and other cases, which means the receiver have to predict the error correction value from the received corrections. Range rate can be up to 800 m/s if get the correction after 1milisec, the bias is large.

   Error types: 
            - Space segment error : Satellite clock error (can be eliminated in differential mode)                                     Satellite ephemeris
            - Atmosphere error : Ionospheric Propagation delay (1-2mm/km, can reach up 1m when the Ionospheric active)            
                            Tropospheric propagation delay
                - Local error : receiver noise and multipath
